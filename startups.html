What founders usually come to us with

“We're raising, and investors want a financial model.”

We build a model you can defend line by line, and a deck that tells the same story. Fundraise support

“We don't know our real burn or runway.”

A monthly MIS with cash and runway front and centre, reviewed with you every month. Virtual CFO

“We have a term sheet. Is it fair?”

We review the terms, the dilution and the structure before you sign. Deal structuring

“Investors are asking for a valuation.”

A valuation built on methods investors recognise, explained so you can negotiate. Business valuation

“Compliance keeps slipping.”

Tax and GST on a fixed calendar, with reminders well before each deadline. Tax compliance

“We're setting up and want the structure right.”

Entity choice, founder shareholding and tax benefits planned from day one. Tax advisory
