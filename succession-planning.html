Succession planning & business transition

For owners planning what happens to their business next: when there's no successor, the next generation has chosen a different path, or it's simply time to step back.

Your business doesn't have to close when you step away. We help you plan the handover, value the business, and find the right next owner, partner or operator, so the business keeps running and you realise the value you've built.

Start a confidential conversationLooking to take over a business?

Ways your business can carry on

  • A full saleTo a buyer from your industry, an investor or a family office.
  • A partner or investorSomeone takes a stake, you take some value out and stay involved.
  • A new operatorA capable person or company takes over the day-to-day running.
  • A management buyoutThe people who already run the business take it over, often in stages.
  • A planned family handoverWhere a relative is willing, a structured transfer with clear ownership.

How we take you through it

  1. Readiness review

    What a buyer will look at, and what needs work first.

  2. Valuation

    What the business is worth, and what you can realistically expect.

  3. Getting buyer-ready

    Books, compliance and documents in order; personal and business assets separated.

  4. Choosing the route

    Sale, partner, operator, buyout or family handover, weighed against your goals.

  5. Finding the right party

    Suitable buyers and partners identified and approached confidentially.

  6. Negotiation and structure

    Price, terms, staged payments and how control passes over.

  7. Tax on the transfer

    Share sale or business sale, capital gains and the most efficient route.

  8. Through the transition

    Due diligence support and a handover plan that keeps the business running.
